Child Safety Standards & Policy

App Name: MyChannel YT

Last Updated: September 2026

Our Commitment: MyChannel YT maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are fully committed to providing a safe environment for all users.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

We strictly prohibit any content, behavior, or interactions that involve child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or the sexual exploitation of minors. Any accounts, channels, or content found to violate these standards will be immediately banned, removed, and reported to the relevant authorities.

2. In-App Reporting Mechanism

We empower our users to help maintain a safe community. If you encounter any suspicious behavior, content, or violations related to child safety within the app, you can easily report it using our in-app reporting feature or contact our support team directly.

3. Reporting to Authorities

In accordance with global child safety laws, we cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and national organizations (such as NCMEC) to report any instances or suspected instances of child sexual abuse and exploitation.
