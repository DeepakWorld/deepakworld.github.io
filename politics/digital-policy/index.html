Government Launches New Digital Policy

Published: September 12, 2026 | Author: Deepak

India announces a new digital transformation policy to empower SMEs. The initiative aims to boost innovation, improve access to technology, and strengthen the digital economy across the country.

This policy will focus on providing subsidies for cloud adoption, training programs for small businesses, and incentives for startups working in AI and cybersecurity.
