Welcome to Deepak IT & Web Services. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect and handle your information when you use our website or Android applications (including MyChannel YT).

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, and phone number when you register or contact us.
  • Transaction Data: Payment details processed securely via PhonePe and Razorpay (we do not store full card/PIN details).
  • Device Information: Model, OS version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and security.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our apps and games, including features used and achievements.

2. YouTube API Services & Channel Analytics

  • Scope of Collection: For applications like MyChannel YT, we utilize YouTube API Services to allow users to view, track, and manage their YouTube channel statistics, metrics, and video performance dashboards.
  • Data Usage: Data accessed via YouTube APIs is used strictly to provide dashboard metrics within the app. We do not cache, store permanently, or sell your private YouTube credentials or channel data to third parties.
  • User Terms: By using our applications that connect with YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access to your data at any time via your Google Security Settings page.

3. Location Data and Background Collection

  • Scope of Collection: Our app may collect location data, including precise and background location information, to enable core features such as Friends-Location and device tracking where applicable.
  • Background Usage: Location data is collected and used even when the app is closed or not actively in use to ensure continuous functionality for these specific features. This data is strictly used for core app features and is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services, tools, and games.
  • Process your transactions and payments via secured payment gateways.
  • Deliver instant digital game or application content.
  • Notify you about updates or technical issues.
  • Enable map-based features, Friends-Location tracking, and device tracking using location data where requested.

5. Prominent Disclosure & Consent

Before collecting location data, our app displays a clear, prominent disclosure explaining why location is needed. You must provide explicit consent before background location tracking begins. You may revoke your consent at any time by disabling location permissions in your device settings.

6. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell your data. Information is only shared with trusted third parties like PhonePe and Razorpay for payment processing, or compliant API platforms like YouTube when using analytics tools. We use industry-standard encryption and reasonable security practices to protect your data.

7. Advertising & Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

8. International Compliance

Deepak IT & Web Services complies with India’s DPDP Act, Google AdSense Program Policies, Google Play developer requirements, and international standards such as GDPR.

9. Your Rights

  • Access a summary of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate personal information.
  • Request the erasure of your data when no longer needed.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

10. Children's Privacy

Our applications and games may be used by families and children, but we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without parental consent. We comply with Google Play's "Families" policies regarding data collection.
